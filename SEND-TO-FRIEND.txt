JARVIS handoff

Send your friend the GitHub link, or this whole folder. He cannot upload this file, the zip, or JARVIS.app to the App Store.

Apple only accepts a Mac app that was signed with a Mac App Store distribution certificate. This copy is signed on one Mac only, so Transporter and App Store Connect will reject it.

Before he can submit anything, these have to be true:

1. The Apple Developer Program membership is active on the account that will own JARVIS. That membership is 99 USD per year.
2. He is invited to that team in App Store Connect, or he is using his own developer account and you are fine with the app living there.
3. The app is rebuilt in Xcode with that team's certificate. The bundle id in the project is com.jarvis.desktop.
4. The rebuild drops the private Apple sensor reader and stops launching a separate Node.js program. The App Store sandbox blocks both.

When that signed build exists, he creates a macOS app in App Store Connect, uploads the build with Apple's Transporter app, and fills in screenshots, a privacy policy, and the age rating.

Until then, people install JARVIS from GitHub:

Windows: jarvis/start-windows.bat
Mac: jarvis/start-mac.command
Linux: jarvis/start-linux.sh

Node.js 20 or newer is required. The page opens at http://127.0.0.1:4173
